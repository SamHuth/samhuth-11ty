### Build your own tools to solve your own problems
There is no need to rely on big tech to create and publish digital applications and tools. The technology and resources are out there to enable you to solve your own problems. Create somethin with less bloat and fewer of those "features" that go unused. You may have doubts about managing something yourself, but the potential benefits far exceed these and open opportunities around your solving your problems. LLM's can be used to upskill and start prototyping. Local LLM systems will empower people and companies far greater than any SaaS product can. That's the power that I want people to realise... it's that you can construct these systems on your own and make them work for you better than any other company who just sees you as a profit generator.
